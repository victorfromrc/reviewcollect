# WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp Business account to Review Collect to send your review requests directly on WhatsApp.

To make the integration easier, Review Collect offers you a new phone number to associate with your WhatsApp account. This number is integrated into your own WhatsApp account, so you see no difference in how you configure and customize the account.

## Before you start

Be logged in to your personal Facebook account in your browser.

Three Meta concepts are worth knowing:

- **Business Portfolio:** the global account that manages all your company's Meta assets. It can contain several WhatsApp Business accounts.
- **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA):** the main account that groups the phone numbers used to talk with your customers on WhatsApp.
- **WhatsApp Profile:** the visible face of your company for customers (name, logo, contact details), tied to a unique phone number.

## Start the connection

1. Go to the **Integrations** page in Review Collect.
2. On the WhatsApp row, click **Connect**.
3. You are redirected to the Facebook connection flow.

Complete the process all the way to the end. Otherwise the integration is not registered and you have to start over.

## Follow the Facebook flow

1. **Continue:** on the first page, click Continue.
2. **Business portfolio:** select your existing business portfolio, or create one.
3. **WhatsApp Business account:** select your existing account, or create one. If you create one, choose an appropriate name: it is used as the display name your customers see. It can be changed later, subject to Meta's processing times.
4. **Phone number:** select "Number provided by the Business Solution Provider (BSP)". This is the number Review Collect provides.
5. **Confirm:** all the configuration steps are done, you just need to confirm.
6. **Loading:** wait for the loading to finish. Do not leave the page.
7. **Finish:** a confirmation page appears. Click the **Finish** button. This click finalizes the integration on the Review Collect side.

You are then redirected to your Review Collect dashboard, where the WhatsApp integration appears as active.

## Recommended: get your business verified by Meta

Verification lets you display your company name in the messages you send, which builds customer trust. It also raises your sending limit from 250 to 1,000 messages per day.

1. Go to the Meta verification page.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Click **Start verification**.

The verification process can take up to 48 hours.

## Recommended: complete your WhatsApp profile

Add your logo and company information to strengthen customer trust and get more reviews.

1. Go to WhatsApp Manager.
2. Select the WhatsApp account linked to Review Collect in the dropdown at the top right.
3. Click the associated phone number, then open the **Profile** section.
4. Fill in your company logo and information, then click **Save**.

## Control your sending volumes

On the **Review Collection > Advanced** page, the **Sending limits** card lets you:

- Limit WhatsApp invites per month. Once the cap is reached, invites fall back to your fallback channel (email or SMS) for the rest of the month. Nothing is blocked.
- Limit invites per day, all channels. The overflow goes out the next day.

With limits disabled, there is no cap.
