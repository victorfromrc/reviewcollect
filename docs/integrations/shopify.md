# Shopify

Connect your Shopify store to Review Collect to trigger your review requests automatically from your orders.

## Connect Shopify

1. Go to the **Integrations** page in Review Collect.
2. Click **Shopify**, then **Connect**.
3. Enter your store's subdomain. It is the part before `.myshopify.com` in your Shopify admin URL. If your admin is `mystore.myshopify.com`, your subdomain is `mystore`.
4. Click **Connect**.
5. You are redirected to Shopify: authorize the connection (OAuth 2.0).

Back in Review Collect, Shopify appears as connected. Your orders now automatically feed your review requests.

## Show product reviews on your store

Review Collect adds three native blocks to your Shopify theme:

| Block | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| Review Collect Reviews list | Product pages |
| Review Collect Star badge | Product pages, under the product title |
| Review Collect All reviews | A page listing all your reviews |

There is no code to paste and no key to configure. Each block finds your Review Collect account from your store automatically. Your Shopify store must be connected to Review Collect first.

## Shopify App Store

Review Collect is also available on the Shopify App Store: https://apps.shopify.com/review-collect
