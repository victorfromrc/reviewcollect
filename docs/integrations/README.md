## Customer tools

- **Klaviyo and Brevo:** sync customer ratings and feedback to your contacts and profiles, and create segments of happy and unsatisfied customers in Brevo.
- **Zendesk and Gorgias:** create support tickets automatically from customer feedback.
- **HubSpot:** show the rating, feedback and review date on your contact records.
- **AfterShip:** send review requests after your orders are actually delivered.
- **Judge.me:** sync your product reviews with Review Collect.
- **BigQuery and Snowflake:** load the history of your invitations into your data warehouse with the [API](../api/README.md).
- **Trustpilot:** reply automatically to your Trustpilot reviews with the Chrome extension.

Step by step guides for each integration are in the help center: https://help.review-collect.com/en/category/integrations-9q9n84/
