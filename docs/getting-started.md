# Getting started

This page walks you through the first steps with Review Collect. Setup takes about 5 minutes, and the configuration can be done the same day.

## 1. Connect your orders

Review requests start from your orders. Connect the source of your customer data:

- **Shopify**: connect your store with a short OAuth flow. See [Shopify](integrations/shopify.md).
- **PrestaShop**: connect with a Webservice key. See [PrestaShop](integrations/prestashop.md).
- **Other sources**: Google Sheets, FTP/SFTP or a custom API integration. See [Other customer data options](integrations/other-data-sources.md).

Review Collect connects to 40+ tools. The help center lists every integration.

## 2. Choose your channels

Review requests can go out by email, SMS, native WhatsApp and QR code. To send on WhatsApp, connect your WhatsApp Business account. See [WhatsApp](integrations/whatsapp.md).

You choose the order of the channels and the fallback. For example, WhatsApp first with email as the fallback.

## 3. Add your Google review link

To collect Google reviews, Review Collect needs the review link of your business profile.

1. Log in with the Google account that owns your Google Business Profile.
2. Open your business profile.
3. Click **See reviews**, then **Get more reviews**. Your review link appears. Copy it.
4. Add it in Review Collect: open the **Review Routing** page, click **Edit**, select **Google My Business**, paste your link and save.

You can also send the link to your account manager, who will set it up for you.

## 4. Decide where happy customers go

On the Review Routing page, you choose where satisfied customers (4 and 5 stars) are directed: Google, Trustpilot and other platforms. See [Showcase reviews](showcase.md).

## 5. Follow the results

Your dashboard centralizes the reviews of all your platforms. See [Understand your reputation](understand.md).
